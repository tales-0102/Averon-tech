Section 01
Brand Overview

A quick snapshot of Lumière Skin before strategy implementation.

Business Type
D2C skincare e-commerce brand, 3 hero products
Description
Minimalist skincare brand built around 3 multi-purpose products instead of a 10-step routine — positioned against routine overwhelm
Target Audience
Women 22–38, urban, skincare-curious but fatigued by complicated routines, values ingredients and simplicity
Brand Positioning
The skincare brand for people who hate skincare routines
Brand Voice
Direct, confident, slightly witty — never preachy or overly clinical
Audience Pain Point
Overwhelmed by 10-step routines, skeptical of ingredient claims, tired of buying products that don't get used

Competitor Analysis

How Lumière Skin is positioned against the competitive landscape.

Competitor A — established DTC brand
Observation
Strong product photography but generic captions.
Gap
no personality or POV — purely transactional content.
Opportunity
Lumière can win on voice and brand personality.
Competitor B — viral TikTok-first brand
Observation

Gap

feels intimidating to skincare beginners.
Opportunity

Section 05
Content Strategy

The content pillars driving Lumière Skin's strategy.

Education
Ingredient myths, skincare simplified, what actually matters
Product proof
Before/after skin texture, real usage demos, AI-enhanced product visuals
Brand personality
Witty takes on skincare culture, relatable routine fails
Social proof
Customer UGC, reviews, real results compilation
Offer
Bundle pushes, limited drops, restock alerts
Section 06
Funnel Strategy

How Lumière Skin moves an audience from first impression to client.

Awareness
Educational reels + witty brand personality posts targeting cold reach
Consideration
Product proof carousels + UGC, retargeted via Story views
Conversion
Bundle offer posts + limited-time CTA stories with swipe-up links
Retention
Restock alerts, loyalty UGC reposts, "how to use it right" follow-up content
Section 07
CTA & Engagement Strategy

How Lumière Skin turns attention into action.

CTA Strategy
Every educational post ends with a save-driving CTA ("save this for your next skincare aisle panic").
Soft-Sell CTA
Every product post ends with a soft-sell CTA ("link in bio, 2 left in stock").
Comment Strategy
Comments are replied to within 30 minutes with personality, not generic "thanks!" responses — replies are treated as their own content opportunity since they're publicly visible.
