Brand Overview

A quick snapshot of Birdie & Wolf before strategy implementation.

Section 03
Audience Research

Strategic audience segmentation behind the Birdie & Wolf content system.

The capsule wardrobe builder
Buys on styling proof
26–34. Wants fewer, better pieces that mix endlessly. Needs to see 5 different outfits from one piece before purchasing.
The recovering fast-fashion shopper
Buys on quality signals
22–29. Tired of cheap hauls that fall apart in a month. Convinced by fabric close-ups and "after 50 washes" durability content.
The effortless dresser
Buys on convenience
28–36. Wants to look put-together without spending time thinking about it. Drawn to outfit formulas that remove decision fatigue.
Section 04
Competitor Analysis

How Birdie & Wolf is positioned against the competitive landscape.

Competitor A — fast-fashion giant
Observation
Huge selection and low prices, but zero brand loyalty — customers churn constantly.
Opportunity
Birdie & Wolf wins on quality and intentionality, not price competition.
Competitor B — sustainable fashion brand
Observation
Strong values messaging but a dry, preachy tone that feels like homework.
Opportunity
Birdie & Wolf can care about quality without lecturing — make "better basics" aspirational, not guilt-driven.
Section 05
Brand Strategy

Brand personality and voice design for Birdie & Wolf.

Personality Traits

Confident · Minimal · Dry-witty · Quietly rebellious

Voice Spectrum

Casual ← → Polished

Birdie & Wolf leans minimal-polished — confident enough to skip the hard sell entirely.

Do

Speak like a friend with great taste who never overexplains.

Don't

Use fast-fashion hype language like "must-have," "trending now," or "everyone's obsessed."

Section 06
Content Strategy

The content pillars driving Birdie & Wolf's strategy.

Styling
Outfit formulas, one piece styled 5 ways, mix-and-match proof
Quality
Fabric, construction, durability — why it costs what it costs
Personality
Founder POV, anti-fast-fashion takes, studio culture
Social proof
UGC, customer styling reposts, real reviews
Offer
New drops, restocks, capsule launches
