Section 03
Audience Research

Strategic audience segmentation behind the Mara Voss — Business Clarity Coach content system.

The overwhelmed founder
Books when she feels understood
30–42. Already has revenue but feels like she's drowning in her own business. Books based on feeling truly seen in a single post, not generic tips.
The side-hustle jumper
Books on social proof
28–38. About to leave a day job, terrified of the leap. Needs to see someone who actually made the jump and survived it before reaching out.
The burned-before client
Books on substance, not promises
32–45. Worked with a coach before who oversold and underdelivered. Highly skeptical of big claims — convinced only by specificity.

Section 05
Brand Strategy

Brand personality and voice design for Mara Voss — Business Clarity Coach.

Personality Traits

Calm · Direct · Warm · Quietly confident

Voice Spectrum

Casual ← → Polished

Mara leans personal and conversational — polished enough to feel credible, never so formal it feels distant.

Do

Speak like a smart friend who's been through it.

Don't

Use hustle-culture language like "crush it," "10x your life," or "unlock your potential."
