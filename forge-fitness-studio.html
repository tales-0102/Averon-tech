Brand Overview

A quick snapshot of Forge Fitness Studio before strategy implementation.

Business Type
Boutique fitness studio — group strength & conditioning classes
Description
A studio built around showing up consistently, not chasing an Instagram-perfect physique
Target Audience
Local adults 25–45 — busy professionals, parents, and people who've tried gyms before and quit
Brand Positioning
Real results, real community — no ego, no gimmicks
Brand Voice
Direct and motivating, never shame-based or toxic-positive
Audience Pain Point
Tired of big-box gyms where nobody notices if they stop showing up, intimidated by influencer-style fitness content

Full Audit Notes

Bio doesn't list the class schedule or a trial offer — the single biggest missed conversion point for a studio like this
Feed mixes random workout clips with no consistent pillar or format, so it feels disorganized rather than energizing
No reels at all, despite workout energy being the highest-performing content format in this niche
No member spotlights or community proof — the page looks empty even when classes are genuinely full
Section 03
Audience Research

Strategic audience segmentation behind the Forge Fitness Studio content system.

The comeback starter
28–45. Has tried gyms before and quit. Needs accountability and checks Instagram to see what a beginner's first class actually looks like.
The busy parent
32–44. Squeezing in a session between work and school pickup. Decides based on how clear the class schedule is and how fast they can get in and out.
The consistency seeker
25–38. Already active but wants structure and community to actually stay consistent. Influenced by member shoutouts and challenge content.
Section 04
Content Strategy

The content pillars driving Forge Fitness Studio's strategy.

The workouts
Class formats, exercises, what a real session actually looks like — the pillar that shows people what they're signing up for.
The community
Member spotlights, group energy, accountability culture — the pillar that makes people want to belong, not just attend.
The coaches
Trainer personalities, coaching philosophy, behind the scenes — the pillar that builds trust before someone ever books a class.
