Section 01
Brand Overview

A quick snapshot of Dana Calloway Realty before strategy implementation.

Business Type
Boutique residential real estate agent, mid-to-upper market
Description
A realtor known for getting listings seen before they're lost in the open-market scroll
Target Audience
Move-up buyers and sellers 35–55, relocating professionals, sellers nervous about timing
Brand Positioning
The agent who sells the story of the home, not just the square footage
Brand Voice
Confident, polished, warm — premium without feeling corporate
Audience Pain Point
Sellers fear their home will sit unsold; buyers are exhausted by listings that all look the same

Full Audit Notes

Listing photos look identical to every other agent's — same angles, same "Just listed!" captions, no story
No video walkthroughs at all, despite video being the highest-performing format in real estate by a wide margin
Bio communicates no specialization or personality — just a name, a brokerage, and a phone number
No social proof anywhere — past sales, sold stories, and testimonials are completely absent
No clear lead-capture mechanism inviting buyers or sellers into an actual conversation
Section 03
Audience Research

Strategic audience segmentation behind the Dana Calloway Realty content system.

The anxious seller · 45–58
Fears
Her home sitting unsold, signaling something's wrong with it
Desires
An agent who treats her sale like the only one that matters
Converted By
Specific recent comparable sales and a clear launch plan, not vague reassurance
The relocating professional · 35–48
Fears
Making a costly mistake while house-hunting remotely
Desires
A knowledgeable guide on the ground, not a pushy salesperson
Converted By
Video content that makes them feel they've already seen the home and neighborhood
The move-up buyer · 38–52
Fears
Overpaying in a competitive, fast-moving market
Desires
Someone who understands their lifestyle, not just their budget
Converted By
Story-driven content showing how past clients found their right home
Section 04
Competitor Analysis

How Dana Calloway Realty is positioned against the competitive landscape.

Competitor A — high-volume team brand
Observation
Posts constantly, but every listing looks identical with no personal connection to any specific agent.
Opportunity
Dana wins by being the visible expert, not hiding behind a brokerage logo.
Competitor B — luxury-focused agent
Observation
Beautiful photography but zero educational content — only attracts buyers who've already decided.
Opportunity
Dana owns the "still thinking about it" audience with story and market-insight content.
Competitor C — aggressive new-agent lead-gen
Observation
High ad spend, low trust signals, little substance behind the pitch.
Opportunity
Dana builds organic trust first, so any paid spend later converts far better.

Funnel Strategy

How Dana Calloway Realty moves an audience from first impression to client.

Awareness
Market-insight reels and neighborhood content reaching cold local audiences
Consideration

Conversion
Direct CTAs ("thinking of selling this year? let's talk numbers") into DM consultations
Retention
Past-client spotlights, home updates, and direct referral asks
Section 07
Listing Launch Playbook

The exact content cadence run for every new listing.

7 Days Out
Teaser content that sells the story, not the address — plus a "coming soon" countdown in Stories.
Launch Day
Full walkthrough reel, listing carousel, and open-house announcement go live together.
Week One
Social proof push showing real interest and showing traffic, with honest urgency framing when it's genuine.
Post-Sale
A "sold" story post and a thank-you to the sellers — content that becomes a trust asset for the next listing.
